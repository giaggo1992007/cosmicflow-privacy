CosmicFlow – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

CosmicFlow is an interactive visual effects application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

CosmicFlow does not collect, request, or transmit personal information. The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal details.

CosmicFlow does not display advertisements and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

4. Sharing of Information

Because CosmicFlow does not collect personal information, it does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

CosmicFlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

6. Data Security and Retention

CosmicFlow does not operate a service that collects or stores users' personal information on the developer's servers.
